17. Narration — Direct & Indirect Speech

प्रत्यक्ष एवं अप्रत्यक्ष कथन | JHTET English Paper II (Upper Primary)

⌂ मुख्य सूची← पिछला अध्याय: Voiceअभ्यास टेस्ट ↓

1. Narration क्या है?

Narration का अर्थ है किसी व्यक्ति के कहे हुए शब्दों को बताना। इसके दो प्रमुख प्रकार हैं:

  • Direct Speech (प्रत्यक्ष कथन): वक्ता के मूल शब्द quotation marks (“ ”) में लिखे जाते हैं।
    Rita said, “I am happy.”
  • Indirect Speech / Reported Speech (अप्रत्यक्ष कथन): वक्ता की बात का अर्थ बताया जाता है; मूल quotation marks हट जाते हैं।
    Rita said that she was happy.
मुख्य भाग: Reporting clause — Rita said; Reported words — “I am happy.”

2. Direct से Indirect बनाने के मुख्य नियम

  1. Quotation marks और सामान्यतः comma हटा दें। जरूरत के अनुसार that, if/whether, wh-word, to आदि लगाएँ।
  2. Pronoun वक्ता, श्रोता और संदर्भ के अनुसार बदलता है—केवल यांत्रिक रूप से नहीं।
  3. यदि reporting verb past tense में है, तो सामान्यतः reported clause का tense एक कदम पीछे जाता है।
  4. समय/स्थान के शब्द संदर्भ के अनुसार बदल सकते हैं; हर स्थिति में बदलना अनिवार्य नहीं।
  5. Sentence का प्रकार पहचानें—statement, question, command/request या exclamation—फिर सही संरचना चुनें।

3. Statements — कथनवाचक वाक्य

Structure: Subject + said/told + (object) + (that) + statement.

Direct SpeechIndirect Speech
He said, “I am tired.”He said that he was tired.
She said, “I have finished my work.”She said that she had finished her work.
Rahul said, “I will help you.”Rahul said that he would help me.
She said to me, “You are kind.”She told me that I was kind.

ध्यान दें: say के साथ object हो तो say to me संभव है; tell के साथ सामान्यतः object चाहिए—tell me, न कि केवल tell।

4. Questions — प्रश्नवाचक वाक्य

Yes/No Questions

Structure: asked + object + if/whether + subject + verb.

DirectIndirect
He said to me, “Are you ready?”He asked me if I was ready.
She asked, “Did you call him?”She asked whether I had called him.

Wh-Questions

Who, what, when, where, why, how जैसे शब्द बने रहते हैं। Indirect question में सामान्य statement word order आता है।

DirectIndirect
He said to me, “Where do you live?”He asked me where I lived.
Mother asked, “Why are you crying?”Mother asked why I was crying.
Indirect question में do/does/did के साथ प्रश्न वाला उलटा क्रम नहीं रहता: “where he lived” सही, “where did he live” गलत (इस संरचना में)।

5. Commands, Requests, Advice और Instructions

Structure: told/ordered/asked/requested/advised + object + to + V1.

DirectIndirect
The teacher said to us, “Sit down.”The teacher told us to sit down.
She said to me, “Please help me.”She requested me to help her.
Father said, “Work hard.”Father advised me to work hard.
He said to me, “Do not touch it.”He warned me not to touch it.

Negative command: not to + V1. Reporting verb चुनते समय वाक्य का भाव देखें—order, request, advice, warning आदि।

6. Exclamations और Wishes

DirectIndirect
She said, “What a beautiful flower!”She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful flower.
He said, “Alas! I have lost my wallet.”He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet.
They said, “Hurrah! We have won.”They exclaimed with joy that they had won.
He said to me, “May you succeed!”He wished that I might succeed.

परीक्षा में भावना के अनुसार exclaimed with joy/sorrow/surprise, wished आदि का प्रयोग किया जा सकता है।

8. कब Tense बदलना जरूरी नहीं?

  • Universal truth: The teacher said, “The Earth revolves around the Sun.” → The teacher said that the Earth revolves around the Sun.
  • वर्तमान में भी सत्य तथ्य: यदि बात अब भी सच है, तो संदर्भ के अनुसार tense को अपरिवर्तित रखा जा सकता है।
  • Reporting verb present/future में हो: सामान्यतः tense backshift नहीं होता। Example: He says, “I am busy.” → He says that he is busy.
  • Past perfect और कई modal verbs का सामान्यतः आगे backshift नहीं होता।

9. Quick Revision — परीक्षा के लिए

  • Statement → (that) + subject + verb.
  • Yes/No question → if/whether + subject + verb.
  • Wh-question → wh-word + subject + verb.
  • Command/request → object + to + V1.
  • Negative command → object + not to + V1.
  • पहले sentence type, reporting verb, pronoun और tense पहचानें।
