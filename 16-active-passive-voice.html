16. Active and Passive Voice
कर्तृवाच्य एवं कर्मवाच्य

रूपांतरण के नियम, tense-wise structures, उदाहरण और 20 MCQs

मुख्य पृष्ठ← पिछला मॉड्यूलअभ्यास टेस्ट ↓

1. Voice क्या है?

Voice से पता चलता है कि वाक्य में subject कार्य करता है या उस पर कार्य किया जाता है।

Active: The teacher teaches English. — शिक्षक अंग्रेज़ी पढ़ाते हैं।
Passive: English is taught by the teacher. — अंग्रेज़ी शिक्षक द्वारा पढ़ाई जाती है।

Active Voice (कर्तृवाच्य): subject कार्य करता है।

Passive Voice (कर्मवाच्य): subject पर कार्य होता है; active का object passive में subject बनता है।

मुख्य सूत्र: Active: Subject + Verb + Object → Passive: Object + suitable form of be + V3 + (by + doer).

2. Active से Passive बनाने के चरण

  1. Active वाक्य में subject, verb और object पहचानें।
  2. Object को passive वाक्य का subject बनाएँ।
  3. Active tense के अनुसार be का सही रूप लगाएँ।
  4. Main verb का past participle (V3) प्रयोग करें।
  5. ज़रूरत हो तो active subject को by + agent के रूप में अंत में लिखें।
  6. Passive subject के अनुसार singular/plural agreement जाँचें।
Active: Rina writes a letter.
Object “a letter” आगे आएगा → A letter is written by Rina.
Passive सामान्यतः उन्हीं verbs के साथ बनता है जिनका object हो (transitive verbs)। Intransitive verbs जैसे arrive, sleep, happen का सामान्य passive नहीं बनता: “He slept” → सामान्य passive नहीं।

3. Tense-wise Passive Structures

TenseActive examplePassive structure / example
Simple PresentShe writes a letter.am/is/are + V3 — A letter is written by her.
Present ContinuousShe is writing a letter.am/is/are being + V3 — A letter is being written by her.
Present PerfectShe has written a letter.has/have been + V3 — A letter has been written by her.
Simple PastShe wrote a letter.was/were + V3 — A letter was written by her.
Past ContinuousShe was writing a letter.was/were being + V3 — A letter was being written by her.
Past PerfectShe had written a letter.had been + V3 — A letter had been written by her.
Simple FutureShe will write a letter.will be + V3 — A letter will be written by her.
Future PerfectShe will have written a letter.will have been + V3 — A letter will have been written by her.
Modal verbsShe can write a letter.modal + be + V3 — A letter can be written by her.
सामान्य परीक्षा-व्याकरण में Present Perfect Continuous, Past Perfect Continuous, Future Continuous और Future Perfect Continuous के passive रूप प्रायः नहीं पूछे जाते या अत्यंत अप्राकृतिक माने जाते हैं। ऐसे वाक्यों को बिना संदर्भ के जबरन passive में न बदलें।

5. Negative और Interrogative वाक्य

Negative

Passive में auxiliary के बाद not लगाया जाता है।

Active: They do not clean the room.
Passive: The room is not cleaned by them.

Interrogative

प्रश्न में passive auxiliary को subject से पहले रखें।

Active: Did she finish the work?
Passive: Was the work finished by her?

Yes/No question: Does he write poems? → Are poems written by him?

6. Imperatives, Modals और दो Objects

Imperative (आज्ञा/अनुरोध)

Open the door. → Let the door be opened.
Please help me. → You are requested to help me. (अर्थ के अनुसार रूपांतरण)

Modal verbs

You must follow the rules. → The rules must be followed (by you).
We should respect elders. → Elders should be respected.

Two objects

कुछ verbs दो objects लेती हैं; passive में किसी एक object को subject बनाया जा सकता है।

Active: The teacher gave me a prize.
Passive 1: I was given a prize by the teacher.
Passive 2: A prize was given to me by the teacher.

7. By-agent कब हटाएँ?

जब काम करने वाला अज्ञात, स्पष्ट, महत्वहीन या सामान्य हो, तो by + agent छोड़ सकते हैं।

Someone stole my bicycle. → My bicycle was stolen.
People speak English here. → English is spoken here.

यदि कर्ता महत्त्वपूर्ण है, तो उसे रखें: The novel was written by Premchand.

परीक्षा रणनीति: Tense न बदलें; केवल voice बदलें। V3 का प्रयोग करें, auxiliary को नए subject के अनुसार मिलाएँ और अर्थ को मूल वाक्य के समान रखें।
