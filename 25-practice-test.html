Section A — Reading Comprehension (प्रश्न 1–5)

Passage

Reading is not simply the act of recognizing printed words. A thoughtful reader connects new information with prior knowledge, asks questions, and checks whether the text supports an inference. For example, when a story describes dark clouds, strong winds, and people closing their windows, a reader may infer that a storm is approaching. However, a good reader also distinguishes an inference from a fact directly stated in the text. Teachers can support comprehension by inviting learners to predict, summarize, discuss vocabulary in context, and cite textual evidence for their answers.
